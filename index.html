Jamaican Vibes Domino Rules

Playing a hand

  • Four players receive seven dominoes each. Player 1 is you, Player 2 is right, Player 3 is opposite, and Player 4 is left.
  • The double-six holder poses 6-6 to open a new match. Match a domino to either open end. If both open ends show the same number, your domino is placed automatically. If the ends differ and your domino fits both, choose the end you want.
  • If you have no legal move, your turn passes automatically. A hand ends when someone plays their last domino or nobody can play.
  • After a normal win, other hands stay hidden. When blocked, all remaining dominoes are revealed and their pips counted; the lowest individual count wins.

Cut Throat — first to 6

  • No partners and no bruck reset. Each player keeps their own running score across hands. For example, 1, 2, 1, 0 stays on the scoreboard as play continues.
  • A normal hand win earns 1 point. The previous hand winner poses the next hand. The first player to reach 6 wins; scores never pass 6 and the match stops there.
  • Blocked tie: when two or more players share the lowest pip count, nobody receives points. Select one of these two versions before play:
  • Play for 2 — double-six poses: the next hand is worth 2 points, and whoever is dealt double-six poses 6-6. Repeated ties keep the next hand worth 2; they do not increase it further.
  • Play for 1 — previous winner poses: the next hand is worth 1 point, and the last winner before the tied hand poses. If no hand has been won yet, double-six poses for 1.
  • After a winner, the following hand returns to 1 point unless another blocked tie occurs.

Partners — Bruck & Start, first to 6

  • Player 1 and Player 3 are partners against Player 2 and Player 4. The first team to reach 6 wins, with no score above 6.
  • Choose who poses after the deal: after an ordinary winning hand, shuffle and deal the next hand first. Then the winning partners choose which partner will pose, using those newly dealt hands. Confirming the choice does not reshuffle or redeal.
  • One-one play two: if one team goes 1 and the other wins the next hand, it is one-one. The next hand plays for 2; the winning team goes 2.
  • BRUCK restart: if a team has a run of 2 or more and the other team wins, everybody goes back to zero. The next hand plays for 1. Whoever receives double-six automatically poses 6-6; the partners do not choose the poser for this restart.
  • Blocked tie across opposing teams: no points are awarded. The next hand plays for 2 and double-six poses. A blocked hand is not automatically a bruck.
  • The same partner scoring applies online and against computer players.

Time, chat and online play

  • Turn timer: 20 seconds with 4–7 dominoes; 30 seconds with 1–3 dominoes. When time expires, a legal domino is played automatically.
  • Computer pace: computer players pause about 2 seconds before a move. Your turn starts immediately when play returns to you.
  • Live voice: seated players can tap Join Voice and allow their microphone to talk with the other players. Use Mute or Leave Voice whenever you want.
  • The host selects the mode and tie version before an online match. All four players must be ready. The server validates moves and keeps each player's hand private.
  • After a connection loss during an active match, the game pauses for up to 90 seconds for reconnection to the same seat and hand. Leaving deliberately or failing to reconnect ends the active match.
  • After the match, all four players can vote for a rematch. A rematch starts a new match in the selected mode with scores reset.
