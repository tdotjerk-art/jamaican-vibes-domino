Privacy Policy

Jamaican Vibes Games · Last updated November 19, 2026

This notice describes information used when you visit or play Jamaican Vibes Domino, LUDI, Jamaican Draff, Jamaican 3 Cards, and Jamaican Texas Hold’em on this site. It also describes advertising that may be enabled after approval.

Information used to operate the games

  • Player names and game information. Names you enter, multiplayer room codes, seats, game actions, scores, and tournament or player-profile records may be processed to run a game, reconnect players, display results, and prevent invalid moves. Your display name and gameplay may be visible to other players.
  • Stored game settings and reconnect credentials. The website may keep your display name, sound preferences, saved game choices, player-profile identifiers, and reconnect tokens in your browser's local storage. These help remember your choices or resume games on the same device.
  • First-party game analytics. The website creates a random browser identifier saved in local storage and a first-party cookie named jv_analytics_browser (with up to 90 days' lifetime). It uses this identifier for visit and play-session statistics across the games. The analytics service hashes identifiers for its reporting data and is designed to retain up to 90 days of daily statistics, although actual storage duration depends on server configuration.
  • Technical data. Hosting providers and other website services may process information such as your IP address, browser details, requests, and error logs to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the site.
  • Optional voice chat. If you choose to turn on in-game voice chat, your browser requests microphone permission and sends audio to other players in the connected session using WebRTC. Network connection data may be exposed as part of establishing peer connections. Do not share sensitive information over voice chat.

How information is used

We use information to deliver game sessions, maintain player profiles and reconnect functionality, show player names and results, detect technical problems, understand game activity, and improve reliability. We do not ask players to post passwords or financial details in the game.

Advertising and cookies

The site has added Google AdSense verification code, but Google site review and advertising permissions may still be pending. If advertising is activated, Google and its partners may use cookies or other identifiers to deliver, measure, and personalize ads as allowed by your settings and applicable law. See Google's advertising technology information and Google's Privacy Policy. Any future rewarded ads will be optional and subject to the applicable program requirements. The presence of verification code does not mean rewarded ads are currently active.

Sharing and service providers

Game information is shared with other participants as needed for multiplayer play. Technical hosting and advertising partners may process information to provide their services. We do not intentionally publish saved room tokens or private account credentials to other players.

Your choices

You can stop playing, decline optional microphone access, and use your browser settings to clear cookies or local storage. Clearing browser storage may remove preferences, remembered names, and saved reconnect credentials, and could prevent resuming an existing game. You can also manage Google's ad personalization settings at My Ad Center.

Children and sensitive information

Please do not enter sensitive personal information in your display name, player messages, or voice chat. Parents and guardians should supervise children's internet use. Games and advertising must comply with any applicable age and consent requirements.

Questions and requests

For a general privacy question, you can contact the site operator through the project's issue tracker. Issues are public. Do not include private information, passwords, authentication tokens, or sensitive data in a public issue. For a private data-access or deletion request, ask for a private contact method without posting personal information.

Changes

We may revise this notice when game features, storage, or advertising change. The date above indicates the last update.
