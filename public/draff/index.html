How to play

Draff with flying kings · 8 × 8 board. Gold moves first. Regular pieces move diagonally forward on dark squares, but can capture diagonally both forward and backward. Captures are optional. If you make a normal move when a capture was available, your opponent may call Huff and remove one piece that could have captured. They must press “Huff” and tap the correct piece before making their next move. If they do not notice, no piece is removed. If the piece moved, claim it on its new square. An incorrect Huff removes nothing. Once a capture starts, continue jumping with that piece when possible. In computer games, the computer may notice missed captures too. Reach the far edge to become a king (♛), ending your turn. Kings slide any number of open squares diagonally, forward or backward. A king captures by crossing one opposing piece and landing on any empty square beyond it; it cannot cross a friendly piece or two pieces in one jump. Continue capturing when possible. Win by taking all opposing pieces or blocking every move. Three repeated positions or 80 turns without a capture or promotion is a draw.
