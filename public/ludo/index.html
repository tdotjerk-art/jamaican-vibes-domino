How to play
  1. Each player has four pieces. Roll a six to move a piece from its yard to its starting square. Green arrows mark the starting squares.
  2. Move around the track in the arrow direction, then up your own home lane. Roll both dice on the table, tap the die you want to use, then choose a piece. Use each die once: move the same piece twice or split the dice between two pieces. You need the exact roll to finish.
  3. There are no safe zones on the shared track. Landing on a lone opponent sends that piece back to its yard, including on arrow starting squares. Entering from your yard also captures a lone opponent on your starting square. A piece that captures stops for the rest of that roll: use the remaining die on a different piece. If no other piece can move, skip that die. Capturing pieces become available again on the next roll.
  4. Two or more of your pieces on one outer-track square form a barrier. Opponents cannot land on it, capture its pieces, or pass it, including on starting squares. You may stack all four pieces together and pass your own barrier. A barrier stays protected while at least two pieces remain; a lone piece can be captured.
  5. Only a six on either die earns another roll after you use the available dice. Capturing an opponent or bringing a piece home does not earn an extra roll. Three consecutive rolls containing a six end your turn before you move on the third roll. If neither die can move a piece, your turn passes automatically. A die that cannot be used is skipped.
  6. Bring all four pieces home to win. Two-player games use opposite corners. No teams: everyone plays for themselves.

Computer turns pause so you can follow the game. Online rooms require 2–4 players and all players must consent to a new game. Your name is remembered on this device; your saved room token recovers your seat. Rooms last up to 24 hours and end when the server restarts or deploys.
