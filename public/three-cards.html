Game activity

    Jamaican 3 Cards

    Use a regular 52-card pack. Everyone picks a card; highest deals. Ace is high in this version. Tied highest players pick again.

    1. The dealer shuffles, deals 3 cards each, then turns up a trump card. A 6 or Jack gives the dealer 1 point per player (3 players = 3 points). Play continues.
    2. Starting to the dealer’s right, a player holding the same rank can play their matching card plus another card from their hand. The turned-up card stays on the table.
    3. The other card played becomes the next rank to match. Chances to use it pass to the right. You can decline.
    4. With just one card left, use a matching table card to play your last card alone and win.
    5. If nobody uses the opening card, the second player to the dealer’s right draws. A draw turns up one card; it does not enter anyone’s hand. The drawer has first choice, then chances pass to the right. If nobody uses it, the next player draws.
    6. The winner gets +1. Everyone else gets −1. The winner deals next.
    7. If the deck runs out, no hand points change, but the dealer keeps their bonus. The game counts; everyone picks again to select a dealer.

    Agree on 5, 10, 15 games or more. At the end, positive scores win; negative scores lose; zero is even. Add more games in steps of 5 by agreement.

    Four players sit at every table: you and three computers, or four online friends. Online play pauses if someone disconnects. Reopen this page on the same browser to reconnect. Tables are currently held in server memory and can be lost when the server restarts.
